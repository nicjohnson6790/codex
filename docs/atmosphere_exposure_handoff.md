# Exposure, sun, space and cloud domain handoff

## Implementation and defaults

Automatic exposure is on by default. The display subsystem owns three compute passes, a 1 KiB histogram and an eight-byte persistent state; no metering readback, CPU fence wait or per-frame allocation was added. Metering follows the complete HDR scene and precedes overlays. The sample grid scales to the viewport aspect ratio within 256×144, without sampling outside valid texels. Rec.709 log luminance spans −20/+20 stops in 256 bins; nonfinite RGB is ignored, black maps to the low endpoint, and percentile trimming retains partial boundary bins. Linear middle gray is 0.18, compensation 0 EV, limits −12/+16 EV, bright/dark time constants 0.5/2 seconds, dt at most 0.1 seconds. GPU validity tracks the first valid frame even after invalid frames. Manual-to-auto seeds from manual exposure; resize preserves state. The existing exponential shoulder and exact sRGB transfer are unchanged.

The directional background now adds the 0.266-degree sun disk to decoded space before applying air transmission once. Its edge uses caller ray derivatives, calculated before the sky depth discard or water TIR branch. Existing artistic solar RGB is multiplied by a named disk-only calibration of 100, capped at 60,000 per channel; the atmosphere/cloud scales 4.8 and 12 are unchanged. This is not a physical irradiance conversion. Planar downward celestial rays encounter the surface; upward space-facing rays remain eligible regardless of the sun center's Y sign. Visible sky and water reflection/non-TIR transmission share this function; ambient remains disk-free. Opaque depth and cloud compositing retain occlusion.

## Space diagnosis

Both runtime skybox files are present and the runtime successfully loads them. The loader resolves px/nx/py/ny/pz/nz, checks square matching dimensions, uploads six faces to R8G8B8A8_UNORM_SRGB, and binds the existing cubemap sampler. GPU sampling supplies the sole sRGB decode. Sky and water use inverse sky rotation; sky rejects opaque depth. Above-atmosphere outward rays retain unit transmission and zero scattering; entering rays retain the existing distance extension and atmospheric integration. Final display conversion occurs once.

The source scale is a proven quantization failure at the previous fixed exposure: decoded cubemap values are at most one, and displayTransfer(0.0001,1) is approximately 0.001292, below 0.5/255. Thus even a white authored texel rounds to black in clear space with no scattering. A numerical regression test covers this. The shared source scale is now 0.02, independent of camera exposure. No asset replacement or night/altitude boost was added. This establishes the calibration failure, not visual approval of every camera scenario or of the new 0.02 setting.

## Loaded clouds and sampling tradeoff

Terrain has nine active roots (3×3). App updates CloudManager from that actual active center; its retained 5×5 set encloses the roots. The existing 81×81 prepared field defines a 2,621,440 m square. Bounds are derived from macro minimum, pitch and dimension, after integer grid subtraction. Shared axis/slab intersection starts at the actual domain entry and supports parallel, outside and away-pointing rays. Fullscreen only clips its exit to opaque scene depth. No atmospheric radial cutoff remains.

Fullscreen count is max(viewSteps, ceil(occupied ray length/2000 m)), capped by the total budget (default 512, configurable 128–1024). Boundaries use length × (0.25u + 0.75u²), preserving finer entry sampling while covering the complete interval. Default view/sun settings remain 48/6. At the longest approximately 3,707 km diagonal the default budget gives gaps up to about 12.7 km, below the 32.768 km macro lattice pitch but coarser than some detail-noise features. Thin distant wisps can alias or disappear between samples; review this explicit detail tradeoff. Raising the budget reduces gaps but increases cost. There is no hidden radial fallback, temporal reconstruction or new residency infrastructure.

Worst-case default work is 512 primary plus 3,072 sunlight density evaluations per pixel, with transmittance and empty-density early exits. Maximum settings bound it to 1,024 × 33 = 33,792 density evaluations. Water retains uniform 24/3 samples at multiplier 0.5 and its explicitly water-only 180 km distance setting; the shared footprint intersection may shorten that interval. Reflections can consequently omit clouds visible beyond 180 km in the fullscreen sky. The fullscreen budget never multiplies water work.

## Validation and remaining acceptance

Canonical Debug and Release builds and all six tests pass. Shared C++/GLSL numerical coverage includes percentile boundaries, black/invalid samples, state seeding, clamps, dt behavior, manual transform parity, sun/ambient separation and cloud domain/parallel/shifted-origin cases. All 37 Debug SPIR-V modules validate. Exposure compute declarations match their shader sets: clear RW histogram; accumulation sampler + RW histogram + uniform; reduction RO histogram + RW state + two vec4 uniform block. Display reads one sampler and one storage buffer. Existing cloud/water block sizes remain 256/320 bytes with C++ assertions.

The 720-frame Debug Vulkan traversal passed all 12 stages and 24 coverage revisions, exercising below/inside/above/horizontal rays, cell crossings, seed changes and universe-scale origins. A subsequent 120-frame Debug run checked the revised GPU validity state, and a final Release startup frame passed. All 37 Release SPIR-V modules also validate. Only the known SDL maintenance9 3D-image compatibility warning appeared; no validation errors were logged. The traversal used coverage 0.6 and camera heights 300/1800/2500/4200/7000/2500 m, cell 23 then 2^55, from the existing verifier. It does not exercise a space-altitude camera or establish distant-corner appearance.

Native screen capture/control is not exposed in this session, so no new visual comparison is claimed. The local registry identifies AMD Radeon(TM) Graphics; the selected Vulkan adapter was not independently captured. Existing profiling measures CPU scopes rather than GPU pass times. Per-pass GPU before/after milliseconds for exposure, grazing/high-altitude clouds and water remain unavailable; operation bounds above are not timing measurements. Do not treat runtime completion as performance acceptance.

User review still needs fixed manual exposure 1 and automatic day/night/ground-to-sky transitions; noon/horizon/terrain/thin-and-thick-cloud sun occlusion; space-facing views above the atmosphere; reflection and underside Snell-window behavior; unchanged overlays; loaded corners beyond 180 km and near detail. A useful fixed baseline is the prior HDR horizon setup: cell (23,−1), local (524250,300,347000), direction normalize(0,−0.05,−1), 15:00, clouds off, wave time 10 s. Also inspect high-altitude oblique/grazing occupied coverage and record GPU pass times at the intended viewport resolution before accepting the 512 budget and new source calibration.
